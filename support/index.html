Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hello@baynestudio.com and we'll get back to you.

01

Something wrong about a bar?

Open the bar in barzi and use Suggest an edit, or find it in Settings. You can also tell us about a bar that's missing. We check every suggestion before it goes live.

02

Run a bar in Hoboken?

If your hours, weekly nights or specials need fixing, email hello@baynestudio.com and we'll update your page.

03

Delete your account

In barzi, go to Profile, Settings, Delete account. Your profile, photo, saved bars, check-ins, suggestions and usage record are deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.

04

Location

barzi uses your location while the app is open to show what's near you and to confirm check-ins. You can turn it off any time in your iPhone's Settings. You can still browse bars, but you can't check in.

05

Where barzi works

barzi is live in Hoboken, NJ. More towns are coming soon.
