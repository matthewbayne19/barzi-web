Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hello@baynestudio.com and we'll get back to you.

01

Something wrong about a bar?

Open the bar in barzi and use Suggest an edit, or find it in Settings. You can also tell us about a bar that's missing. We check every suggestion before it goes live.

02

Run a bar in Hoboken?

See For bars below for barzi for bars, where you keep your bar’s page up to date yourself. Or email hello@baynestudio.com and we'll update it for you.

03

Delete your account

In barzi, go to Profile, Settings, Delete account. Your profile, photo, saved bars, check-ins, suggestions and usage record are deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.

04

Location

barzi uses your location while the app is open to show what's near you and to confirm check-ins. You can turn it off any time in your iPhone's Settings. You can still browse bars, but you can't check in.

05

Where barzi works

barzi is live in Hoboken, NJ. More towns are coming soon.

For bars

barzi for bars

Help for bars that manage their page at bars.gobarzi.com. Stuck on anything? Email hello@baynestudio.com.

B1

Getting a login

We set up logins after talking with someone at the bar, so there’s no sign-up form. Want one for your bar? Email hello@baynestudio.com.

B2

Signing in

  • Go to bars.gobarzi.com. Your phone is fine.
  • Enter the email we set up for you.
  • We email you a 6-digit code from bars@gobarzi.com. Type it in, or tap the link in the email on the same device.

No code? Check spam and promotions, and make sure it’s the exact email we set up. Codes work once and expire, so ask for a new one if it’s been a while.

B3

What you can change

  • Nights and events: weekly trivia, karaoke, live music, bingo, one-off events.
  • Specials: happy hour, food nights, brunch.
  • Hours and closures: weekly hours, and “closed until” for holidays or renovations.
  • Flyers: for a night or a date. They come down on their own after.
  • Features: pool, darts, patio, dog-friendly and more.
  • Details: phone, website, Instagram, food, TVs, sports and team bar.

Changes show up in the barzi app right away.

B4

What stays with us

Your bar’s name, address, map pin, bar type and price level. Email hello@baynestudio.com if one is wrong.

B5

Made a mistake?

Fix it in the portal, or email hello@baynestudio.com. We keep a record of every change and can put things back.

B6

Adding someone from your team

Email hello@baynestudio.com with their name and email and we’ll set them up. Same if someone leaves and their login should be turned off.
